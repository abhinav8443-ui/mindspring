MindSpring is a full-stack, free-to-use **student mental wellness platform** specifically crafted for students preparing for high-pressure competitive exams such as **JEE Main & Advanced, NEET-UG, UPSC CSE, CA Foundation**, and state board examinations.

> **Our Core Philosophy**: **MindSpring is solely made for students' mental wellbeing, not studies.** When students visit MindSpring, they are entering a calm, judgment-free sanctuary — they do not need to study or face academic pressure here. While the platform includes an optional schedule and exam deadline tracker to keep track of key dates without mental clutter, this is deliberately placed as the **last option** in the navigation menu.

The platform provides students with an interactive **AI Wellbeing Companion**, **4 Interactive Wellbeing Games**, a **Mental Wellness Sanctuary**, an **Ethical Safeguarding & Trusted Adult Network**, and a directory to keep **Teachers' & Academic Mentors' Contact Info** readily available whenever guidance is needed.

## 🌟 Key Pillars & Features

- **Palette**: Soft lavender (`#FAF5FF`, `#A855F7`), restorative sage green (`#F2F9F5`, `#3D9A6F`), clean white, and subtle sky blue (`#F0F9FF`, `#0284C7`).
- Rounded cards, tasteful shadows, smooth micro-animations, clear typography (Plus Jakarta Sans).
- Responsive layout with desktop sidebar and mobile navigation drawer.
- Accessible form validation, empty states, loading indicators, and celebration confetti for completed milestones.

  - **Admin**: Safeguarding & Compliance Officer

### 3. Student Mental Wellness Sanctuary (Zero-Pressure Landing Page)
When students open MindSpring, they land on a peaceful sanctuary designed to decompress tension:
- **Interactive 60s Vagus Breathing Orb**: Rhythmic, visual inhale/hold/exhale pacing with live progress timer.
- **Ambient Soundscapes Generator**: Synthesizes Gentle Rain, Tibetan Singing Bowl, and Forest Breeze via native Web Audio API oscillators.
- **Daily Mood & Energy Check-in**: 5-point emotional status selector with supportive affirmations.
- **AI Companion Quick Lounge**: Quick conversation prompt cards to chat with their chosen companion.
- **Daily Mindful Affirmations & Grounding Tips**.
- **Optional Schedule Access**: A gentle, subtle link pointing to the study schedule as the last option if they ever wish to reference exam dates.
- **Strict Separation**: Students are **never ranked by mental health scores**.

### 4. Mental Wellbeing Reflection (Non-Diagnostic)
- **Weekly Reflection Questionnaire**: Adapted responsibly from validated youth instruments (PHQ-A / GAD-7) into 7 student-friendly dimensions: mood, stress, sleep, energy, social connection, academic pressure, and access to support.
- **Wellbeing Harmony Index (0–100)**: Non-clinical, supportive metric designed solely for self-awareness.
- **Historical Check-in Trends**: Visual tracking of recovery rhythms over weeks.
- **Private Encrypted Journaling**: Zero-knowledge client reassurance. Students can freely record gratitude or exam worries. **Entries are strictly shielded from parents and teachers**.
- **Ethical Safeguard**: Never claims that an AI or questionnaire can diagnose clinical depression or predict suicide. If severe pressure is detected, gentle suggestions to consult real counsellors are surfaced.

### 5. AI Wellbeing Companion (Counselling Chatbot)
Located in the Support section, this interactive companion offers compassionate, context-aware dialogue tailored to students' needs:
- **Selectable Character Styles**:
  1. **Supportive Mentor**: Calm, empathetic, grounded guidance.
  2. **Champion Spirit (Inspired by Cristiano Ronaldo)**: High energy, relentless discipline, resilience, and turning pressure into fuel ("*Siuuu! Keep your head up, champion! Consistent habits beat temporary setbacks...*").
  3. **Cheerful Pal (Inspired by Mickey Mouse)**: Playful, warm, heartwarming problem solver ("*Oh boy, pal! Every tough physics question is just a clubhouse mystery to solve together!*").
  4. **Inspiring Anime Sensei**: Honorable training-arc philosophy, focus, and inner perseverance.
  5. **Calm, Thoughtful Friend**: Quiet listening ear, zero judgment, validates heavy emotions.
  6. **Funny Sidekick**: Witty humor that deflates coaching tension.
  7. **Wise Teacher**: Structured pedagogical breakdowns and conceptual pacing.
  8. **Custom Companion**: Student can input their preferred character name, personality style, speaking tone, and fictional or historical inspiration (e.g. *Tony Stark*, *Sherlock Holmes*, *Gentle Grandmother*).
- **Selectable Conversation Goals**:
  - Exam stress & panic
  - Feeling lonely / isolated
  - Motivation and confidence
  - Academic burnout & fatigue
  - Managing difficult emotions
  - Taking a calming break
  - General check-in
- **Dynamic Conversation Starters**: Updates automatically based on the chosen goal.
- **Safety Guardian & Distress Override**:
  - If a message indicates acute distress or self-harm keywords, the companion immediately prioritizes safety.
  - Renders a glowing **Crisis Support Card** with direct one-click calling to verified Indian helplines:
    - **Tele-MANAS** (Govt. of India): `14416` / `1800-891-4416` (24x7, Toll-Free, 20+ Languages)
    - **KIRAN Mental Health Helpline**: `1800-599-0019` (24x7, Toll-Free)
    - **Vandrevala Foundation**: `+91 9999 666 555`
    - **AASRA Suicide Prevention**: `+91 98204 66726`
    - **Emergency**: `112`
  - Reassures the student and prompts connecting with a trusted adult.

### 6. Four Interactive Wellbeing Games / Mindful Activities
1. **Pattern & Focus**: Working-memory & cognitive focus activity using a 4-petaled harmonic lotus. Synthesizes calming harmonic chimes using the browser's Web Audio API. Non-punitive and soothing.
2. **Emotion Garden**: Emotional reflection activity where students identify what they are holding (Overwhelmed, Anxious, Hopeful, Exhausted), write a compassionate counter-thought, and plant virtual blossoms (Lavender of Peace, Golden Sunflower, White Lotus). Features an interactive "Water Garden" button with ambient rain sound.
3. **Choices & Stories**: Interactive branching narrative scenarios depicting realistic exam dilemmas (e.g., *The Midnight Clock Before Mock Test #4*, *The Comparison Trap at the Family Dinner*). Offers psychological insights and resilience badges.
4. **Calm Break**:
   - **Guided Box Breathing**: 4s Inhale, 4s Hold, 4s Exhale, 4s Hold with animated pulsing circle.
   - **5-4-3-2-1 Sensory Grounding Checklist**: Breaks acute panic by reconnecting with the five senses.
   - **Ambient Soundscapes Generator**: Synthesizes Gentle Rain, Tibetan Singing Bowl, Ocean Waves, and Forest Breeze purely through native Web Audio API oscillators and filters (zero external audio files required!).

### 7. Academic Preparation Tracker
- Pre-configured syllabus templates for JEE, NEET, and UPSC.
- Daily & weekly study planner with task check-off and time blocks.
- **Spaced Repetition Scheduler**: Ebbinghaus forgetting curve intervals (Day 1, 3, 7, 21 review cycles).
- **Mock Test Tracker & Mistake Notebook**: Categorizes errors (*Conceptual Error*, *Calculation Mistake*, *Silly Error*, *Time Crunch*) with key takeaway formulas to review before exam day.
- **Backlog Buster**: Prioritizes backlog chapters by urgency (High, Medium, Low) with estimated recovery hours.

### 7. Trusted Circle, Counsellors & Teachers' Contact Info
- **Student's Teachers & Academic Mentors**: A dedicated section where students can add and manage contact information for their teachers and academic guides (phone number, email, doubt-clearing hours, and personal mentor guidance notes). Offers instant one-click Call and Email actions.
- **Trusted Adult Designation**: Designate a trusted adult (Mother, Father, Guardian, Mentor) with verified contact details for emergency reassurance.
- **Certified Counsellor Directory**: Profiles of licensed clinical psychologists and adolescent counsellors (e.g., Dr. Ananya Roy, M.Phil NIMHANS) with bio, languages, and ratings.
- **Confidential Booking Modal**: Direct student-controlled appointment requests.
- **Ethical Safeguarding Protocol**: Documents consent policies, minimum-necessary data access, and protocols for when home is not a safe contact (directing escalation to neutral child-protection bodies like Childline 1098).

### 8. Study Schedule & Exam Deadlines (Optional — Last Navigation Option)
Placed as the **last option in the sidebar**, this tool is available strictly if students wish to organize their schedule without academic pressure:
- Pre-configured syllabus templates for JEE, NEET, and UPSC.
- Daily & weekly schedule planner with low-friction task tracking.
- **Spaced Repetition Scheduler**: Ebbinghaus forgetting curve intervals (Day 1, 3, 7, 21 review cycles).
- **Mock Test Tracker & Mistake Notebook**: Categorizes errors (*Conceptual Error*, *Calculation Mistake*, *Silly Error*, *Time Crunch*) with key takeaway formulas to review before exam day.
- **Backlog Buster**: Prioritizes backlog chapters by urgency (High, Medium, Low) with estimated recovery hours.

### 9. Parent & Counsellor Dashboards
- **Parent View**: Authorized study consistency streak and milestones, transparent privacy assurance regarding private journals, and "Parent Guide to Exam Stress" (guidance on healthy dialogue vs. comparison traps).
- **Counsellor / Admin View**: Authorized referrals, aggregate wellness metrics, and safeguarding logs. (Note: Student monitoring teacher dashboard has been intentionally removed in favor of student-controlled teacher contacts).

### 10. Privacy, Security & Responsible AI
- Aligned with the **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act of India**.
- Explicit consent for collecting wellbeing data.
- Data export (JSON) and complete account data purge features.
- Server-side data access audit logs.
- Disclaimers on all pages clarifying that MindSpring is not a clinical medical facility or crisis hospital.

## 🚀 Quick Start Guide

### Prerequisites
- **Node.js**: v18.0.0 or higher (v24 tested)
- **npm**: v9.0.0 or higher

### Step 1: Clone or Navigate to Project
```bash
cd "C:\Users\Abhinav\.gemini\antigravity\scratch\mindspring"
```

### Step 2: Install Dependencies
```bash
npm run install:all
```
*(Or install separately inside `server` and `client` via `npm install`)*

### Step 3: Run the Full Application

#### Option A: Production Mode (Single Command on Port 5000)
Build the frontend and start the Express server (serves both API and client):
```bash
npm run build:client
npm start
```
Open **`http://localhost:5000`** in your browser.

#### Option B: Development Mode (Hot-Reloading)
Run backend and frontend concurrently in two terminals:

**Terminal 1 (Backend on port 5000):**
```bash
npm run dev:server
```

**Terminal 2 (Frontend on port 3000):**
```bash
npm run dev:client
```
Open **`http://localhost:3000`** in your browser.

## 🔑 External Integrations & Activation

MindSpring works **100% out-of-the-box in demo mode** without external credentials. To graduate to full external production services, configure the following:

| Service | Environment Variable | Current State | How to Activate |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Google Gemini API** | `GEMINI_API_KEY` | Using local high-fidelity persona simulation | Get an API key from [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/) and paste into `.env` |
| **PostgreSQL Database** | `DATABASE_URL` | Using in-memory persistent store | Install PostgreSQL, create database `mindspring`, and supply connection string |
| **Emergency SMS Gateway** | `SMS_GATEWAY_API_KEY` | Audit log record generated | Connect Twilio or Indian DLT-registered SMS provider for crisis alerts to trusted adults |
| **Tele-MANAS Direct Bridge**| `TELE_MANAS_INTEGRATION` | Direct click-to-call modal (`14416`) | Partner with state tele-health nodal centres for human triage dispatch |

## 🛡️ Safety & Safeguarding Limitations

- **Not a Diagnostic Tool**: MindSpring is an educational and self-care accompaniment platform. It does not provide medical diagnoses, clinical prescriptions, or suicide predictions.
- **Human-in-the-Loop**: All clinical evaluations and safeguarding decisions must be conducted by qualified human professionals.
- **Crisis Helpline Escalation**: In immediate physical danger or acute crisis, students and parents should contact **112** (Emergency) or **14416** (Tele-MANAS) immediately.
